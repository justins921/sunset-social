50/50 checklist — how to fill it out

Purpose: we’re counting who takes part in the 50/50 through the 2027 season so we can build fair participation incentives for 2028. This sheet is only a tally — it never touches the money that goes in the treasurer’s register.

The one rule that matters: in a golfer’s cell, write the dollars that person actually put in that week. Blank means they did not buy. That’s it — everything below is just the details.

Solo buy-in

  • Standard amount: write the dollar amount in that week’s cell — e.g. 5. (Even if it’s always the same, writing the number every week is best; a tick alone works only if you note the $ whenever it changes.)
  • Extra tickets: write the real dollars — 10, 15, and so on.

Joint / group buy-in (a pair or group sharing one ticket or one win)

  • On each partner’s row, write the dollars that person put in, then a small + and the partner’s initials — e.g. 5+ML on Alan Lloyd’s row and 5+AL on Mike Lloyd’s row.
  • Never put the whole pot on both rows. Each row shows only that golfer’s share.
  • A joint ticket still counts as a full night for every name on it. It just doesn’t double the dollars.

Other marks

  • Did not play the 50/50: leave the cell blank — blank means “no,” not zero.
  • Absent from golf that week: optional slash / through the cell, so a blank there isn’t read as “chose not to buy.”
  • Winner that week: circle the cell or add a W. This does not change their $ In — the win is cash paid that night.

The three totals on the right

  • Nights — count the weeks with any entry (solo or joint).
  • $ In — add up the dollars you wrote across the row.
  • Shared — count the nights with a “+” (on a joint ticket).

Weekly totals row

  • Buyers this week — how many golfers bought in (count the joint players individually).
  • $ collected this week — the whole pot; roughly 2× the league-half deposit.
  • Running columns — add each week onto the last so you can see the year climb.

Do not record here

  • The winner’s payout and the league-half deposit stay in the treasurer register, not on this sheet.
